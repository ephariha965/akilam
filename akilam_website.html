About Akilam

An offshoot of Sprout Knowledge Solutions, built to bridge talent and transformation.

Akilam Innovations Private Limited is an emerging organization established as an offshoot of Sprout Knowledge Solutions Pvt. Ltd., with a broader vision of building a strong presence across both product-based and service-based business domains.

Built upon the foundation of skill development and industry-oriented training, we bridge the gap between talent development and evolving workforce requirements through structured deployment models and technology-driven solutions.

By leveraging the educational and training ecosystem established through Sprout, we build a sustainable talent pipeline that combines technical competency, operational efficiency, adaptability, and continuous learning.
